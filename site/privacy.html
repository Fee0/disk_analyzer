2. In short

3. Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is served by GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, or, for users in the European Economic Area, GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands. When you open the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the address requested, and details of your browser and operating system. I have no access to these logs. GitHub processes them under its own responsibility and decides how long they are kept.

The legal basis for serving the website through GitHub Pages is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to provide the website reliably and securely.

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR. For details, see GitHub's privacy statement.

The domain's name resolution (DNS) runs through Cloudflare. No website content is delivered through Cloudflare.

4. Looking up the current version

So that the download buttons always point to the newest version and show the right file size, your browser queries GitHub's API (api.github.com) when the page loads. In doing so, your browser necessarily sends GitHub your IP address, the date and time, details of your browser, operating system and language settings, and the address of this website. No cookies are set.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to always offer the current version for download. Section 3 applies to the provider and the transfer to the USA.

5. Detecting your operating system

To suggest the right download, a script in your browser reads your operating system and processor architecture. This information is evaluated only locally in your browser and is neither transmitted nor stored (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

6. Downloads

The program files are stored on GitHub's servers. When you click a download button, your browser fetches the file directly from GitHub; section 3 applies accordingly.
