Questions and answers

Is Disk Analyzer free?

Yes. Disk Analyzer is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no paid tier, no account and no ads.

Is it a DaisyDisk alternative for Windows and Linux?

Disk Analyzer is inspired by DaisyDisk and shows the same kind of sunburst chart, but it runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. It also has an icicle chart and a treemap, the view you may know from WinDirStat.

Is it safe to delete files with it?

Nothing is deleted while you explore. You collect the items you want gone, review the list, and confirm. They move to the Trash on macOS and Linux or the Recycle Bin on Windows, so you can restore them. System locations cannot be collected at all.

Does it send my files or file names anywhere?

No. The app has no network access, no telemetry and no accounts. Every scan stays on your computer.

Why does it show a different size than Finder or Explorer?

Disk Analyzer counts the space a file really takes on disk, not its nominal length. Hard links and cloned copies count once, and space the scan cannot see, such as snapshots and purgeable space, gets its own segment.

How do I scan my whole disk?

On macOS, give the app Full Disk Access in System Settings under Privacy & Security. On Windows and Linux, run it as administrator or root. Folders it cannot read are counted as hidden space.
