The tour

Scan, explore, clean up

Scan a drive, then dive in. Switch between sunburst, icicle and treemap, colour by age, search, and collect what can go.

    Features

    Every byte, accounted for

    Built in Rust for speed. A scan uses every core, and the numbers are the space files really take on disk.

    Scans on every core

    Scan whole disks or single folders, several at once. Pause or stop each scan from its row.

    Sunburst chart

    Each ring is one folder level, coloured by file type. Hover a segment for its size and share.

    Icicle chart

    The sunburst unrolled into rows, with room for names and sizes, so deep folders stay readable.

    Treemap

    Every file as a rectangle sized by the space it takes, nested in labelled folders. The giants stand out.

    Colour by age

    Warm for recent, cold for years old. Click an age to find what nobody has touched in two years.

    True sizes

    Counts the space files use on disk, not their nominal length. Hard links and cloned copies count once.

    Hidden space

    Snapshots, purgeable space and unreadable folders get their own segment, with how to free them.

    Search

    Type a name to narrow the list. The chart lights up where the matches are.

    Clean up safely

    Collect files to delete and see your disk without them first. System locations can't be collected.

    Made for the keyboard

    Fly through folders

    Local only. No accounts, no telemetry, no network access.
    Fresh every time. No cache to go stale: each launch scans anew.
    Native on all three. Trash or Recycle Bin, Finder or Explorer, ⌘ or Ctrl.
